§1 Summary

The short version, if you read nothing else:

In plain terms

Your expense data stays on your device by default. Signing in and turning on cloud backup is optional — you can use every core feature of Ledger Lens without ever creating an account.

Receipt photos are never saved. Text recognition happens on-device using Google ML Kit, then the photo is discarded immediately — it's never stored, uploaded, or synced, by us or anyone else.

We never sell your data. The only outside parties involved are the infrastructure providers (Google Firebase) that make sign-in, backup, and crash/usage diagnostics possible — detailed in §5.

§3 Receipt photos & OCR

When you scan a receipt, the photo is processed entirely on your device using Google's ML Kit text recognition library. The image is never uploaded to Ledger Lens's servers, Google's servers, or anyone else's — the recognition model runs locally, the same way your phone's camera app recognizes a face without sending your photo anywhere.

The recognized text is used once, by a parsing step that guesses the total, merchant, and date, all of which you review and can correct before anything is saved to your ledger. The photo itself is deleted right after that OCR pass — it's never kept, on your device or anywhere else.

§4 Cloud backup & sync

Signing in is entirely optional and is never required to use Ledger Lens's core features. If you choose to sign in with Google, your expense and budget records are additionally stored in Google Firestore (part of Google's Firebase platform), scoped to your account, so they survive a reinstall or carry over to a new device.

Receipt photos are never part of this sync — as described in §3, they're not saved at all, so there's nothing to sync. Only structured expense data (amount, category, date, etc.) is backed up.

You can sign out at any time from Settings. Signing out stops further syncing but does not delete data already saved to your account — see §6 for how to request deletion.

§5 Sharing & processors

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. The only outside organization involved in handling any of this is Google, acting as our infrastructure provider through Firebase:

  • Firebase Authentication — handles the Google Sign-In flow itself; we never see or store your Google password.
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your synced expense/budget records if you're signed in, access-restricted to your account only by server-side security rules.
  • Firebase Analytics — collects the behavioral usage events described in §2, aggregated and anonymized in Google's reporting tools.

Google processes this data on our behalf, under Google's own privacy policy and data processing terms. We do not have any other third-party integrations at this time.

§6 Retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account (or, if you never sign in, your local install) exists. You can delete any individual expense or budget directly in the app at any time — this removes it from your device immediately, and from Firestore shortly after if you're signed in.

To delete your account and all associated data entirely, email us at naveeshaanthony96@gmail.com from the address associated with your account — we'll confirm once it's done. A fully self-service deletion option inside the app is planned for a future update.

§7 Security

Data in transit between the app and Firebase is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Synced records in Firestore are protected by security rules that restrict every account's data to that account alone — no other user, signed in or not, can read or write it. Locally, your data lives in your device's normal app storage, protected the same way any other app's data is on your phone.

§8 Your rights

Wherever you are, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your data — and in Ledger Lens, you largely don't need to ask us for most of it:

  • Access & correction — everything you've entered is visible and editable directly in the app (tap any entry in History to edit or delete it).
  • Deletion — delete individual entries in-app any time, or request full account deletion per §6.
  • Portability & export — not yet built; if you need your data in the meantime, email us and we'll help directly.

If you're in the EU/UK, these map to your rights under GDPR; if you're a California resident, to your rights under the CCPA. We apply the same practices to everyone regardless of where you live.

§9 Children's privacy

Ledger Lens is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us at the email below and we'll delete it.

§10 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that meaningfully affects what we collect or how we use it, we'll update the effective date above and, where required, notify you in-app. Otherwise, this page always reflects our current practice — check back if you're curious.
