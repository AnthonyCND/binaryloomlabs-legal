Delete your account and data

This page explains how to permanently delete your Livestock Keeper account and the data tied to it.

Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open Settings
    In Livestock Keeper, go to the Settings tab and find the Your data section.
  2. Tap "Delete account and data"
    Read what will be erased and confirm. You'll be asked to confirm your Google account once more.
  3. That's it
    Your cloud records, your sign-in account and the data on your phone are erased straight away.

Or ask us to do it

If you can't open the app, email naveeshaanthony96@gmail.com from the address on your Livestock Keeper account saying "please delete my Livestock Keeper account and data". We confirm the request matches your account, delete it, and email you once it's done — within 30 days of your request.

What gets deleted

  • Every farm and record synced to your account: animals, batches, weight, milk, meat, health and breeding records, transactions, categories, budgets, targets and reminders
  • Your account profile (name, email, profile photo from Google Sign-In)
  • Your sign-in itself — you'd need to create a new account to back up to the cloud again
  • All data stored on your phone by the app
Nothing is retained after deletion completes. We don't keep backups of deleted account data beyond what's needed to process the deletion itself.

What this doesn't affect

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Settings → Subscriptions) so you aren't billed again. Subscription purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat follow their own retention policies; email us if you'd like your RevenueCat customer record removed too. If you never signed in, the app only stores data on your phone, and deleting it in the app (or uninstalling) removes it — no email needed.
