Livestock Keeper Privacy Policy

Livestock Keeper is a farm record-keeping app for herds and flocks. This page explains, plainly, what it collects, what it does with that, and what choices you have — written to match exactly what the app does, not a generic template.

§1 Summary

The short version, if you read nothing else:

In plain terms

Your farm records stay on your device by default. Signing in with Google and turning on cloud backup is optional — you can use every core feature of Livestock Keeper without ever creating an account.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app has no advertising and no usage-analytics or crash-reporting service. We don't know which screens you open.

We never sell your data. The only outside parties involved are Google (sign-in and backup) and RevenueCat with Google Play (subscriptions) — detailed in §5.

§3 Cloud backup & sync

Signing in is entirely optional and is never required to use Livestock Keeper's core features. If you choose to sign in with Google, your farm records are additionally stored in Google Firestore (part of Google's Firebase platform), scoped to your account, so they survive a reinstall and carry over to a new device.

You can sign out at any time from Settings. Signing out stops further syncing but does not delete data already saved to your account — see §6 for how to delete it.

§4 Subscriptions & payments

The app is free to use, with optional paid plans (Plus and Pro) bought as subscriptions through Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details — Google Play handles the payment.

To know which plan you have, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives your purchase information from Google Play and a customer identifier — your account ID if you're signed in, otherwise a random anonymous ID — and returns which features you're entitled to. You can manage or cancel a subscription any time in Google Play (Settings → Subscriptions).

If you move to a lower plan or your subscription ends, nothing you've recorded is deleted — records over a plan's limits stay in the app and stay editable; you just can't add more beyond the limit until you upgrade.

§5 On your device

Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself, and your records live in the app's normal private storage. The reports and exports (CSV and PDF) you create are generated on your device and only leave it if you choose to share them, using your phone's share sheet. We never receive them.

§6 Sharing & processors

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. The outside organizations involved in handling any of this, each acting as an infrastructure provider on our behalf, are:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — handles the Google Sign-In flow itself; we never see or store your Google password.
  • Cloud Firestore (Google) — stores your synced farm records if you're signed in, access-restricted to your account only by server-side security rules.
  • Google Play — takes payment for and records your subscription.
  • RevenueCat — verifies your subscription and which plan you have, as described in §4.

These providers process data under their own policies: Google's privacy policy, Firebase data processing terms and RevenueCat's privacy policy. We do not have any other third-party integrations at this time.

§7 Retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account (or, if you never sign in, your local install) exists. You can delete any record in the app at any time — this removes it from your device immediately, and from Firestore shortly after if you're signed in.

To delete everything, open Settings → Your data → Delete account and data in the app. It permanently erases your farms and records from the cloud, deletes your sign-in account, and wipes the data on your device. If you aren't signed in, it erases the data on your device. You can also email naveeshaanthony96@gmail.com from the address on your account, or see the delete account page.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel that in Google Play. Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat are kept under their own policies and the financial-record rules that apply to them; to ask RevenueCat to remove your customer record, contact us and we'll arrange it.

§8 Security

Data in transit between the app and Firebase is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Synced records in Firestore are protected by security rules that restrict every account's data to that account alone — no other user, signed in or not, can read or write it. Locally, your data lives in your device's normal app storage, protected the same way any other app's data is on your phone.

§9 Your rights

Wherever you are, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your data — and in Livestock Keeper, you largely don't need to ask us for any of it:

  • Access & correction — everything you've entered is visible and editable directly in the app.
  • Deletion — delete individual records in the app any time, or delete your whole account and data as described in §7.
  • Portability & export — Settings → Export data creates CSV files of your records you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.

If you're in the EU/UK, these map to your rights under GDPR; if you're a California resident, to your rights under the CCPA. We apply the same practices to everyone regardless of where you live.

§10 Children's privacy

Livestock Keeper is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us at the email below and we'll delete it.

§11 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that meaningfully affects what we collect or how we use it, we'll update the effective date above and, where required, notify you in-app. Otherwise, this page always reflects our current practice — check back if you're curious.
