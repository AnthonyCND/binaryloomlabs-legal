Delete your account and data

This page explains how to request that your Ledger Lens account and the data tied to it be permanently deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from your account's address
    Send a request to naveeshaanthony96@gmail.com from the same email address associated with your Ledger Lens account (the one you signed in with).
  2. Say what you want deleted
    A simple "please delete my Ledger Lens account and data" is enough — no specific subject line or form required.
  3. We verify and confirm
    We confirm the request matches your account, then delete it. You'll get an email once it's done — within 30 days of your request.

What gets deleted

  • Every expense and budget record synced to your account
  • Any custom categories you created
  • Your account profile (name, email, profile photo from Google Sign-In)
  • Your sign-in itself — you'd need to create a new account to use Ledger Lens again
Nothing is retained after deletion completes. We don't keep backups of deleted account data beyond what's needed to process the deletion itself.

What this doesn't affect

Ledger Lens works fully on-device without an account. If you just want to clear data on this phone — not delete a cloud account — you can delete any expense or budget directly in the app any time, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored locally. Neither of those requires emailing us.
